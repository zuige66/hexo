### 2026.6-1 无人机产业

#### 【中文原题】

近年来，中国无人机产业迅猛发展，出口量位居全球首位。中国无人机凭借其出色的稳定性、高清成像能力和便捷的操作性，深受全球消费者的青睐，在民用领域得到了广泛应用。作为国家战略性新兴产业，无人机正逐步演变为智能助手，满足多样化场景的需求。在农村地区，无人机能够精准识别农作物病虫害，并高效喷洒农药。在城市，它们能够快速配送新鲜农产品。在不久的将来，无人机将在更多领域释放独特价值，为经济和社会进步注入新的动力。

#### 【参考译文】

In recent years, China's drone industry has developed rapidly, with its export volume ranking first worldwide. Boasting outstanding stability, high-definition imaging capability and easy operation, Chinese drones are highly popular among global consumers and widely applied in civilian sectors. As a national strategic emerging industry, drones are evolving into intelligent assistants suitable for diverse scenarios. In rural areas, drones can accurately detect crop diseases and pests and spray pesticides efficiently; in cities, they can deliver fresh farm produce quickly. In the near future, drones will generate unique value in more fields and inject new momentum into economic and social development.

#### 【逐句拆解】

**第 1 句**：近年来，中国无人机产业迅猛发展，出口量位居全球首位。
- 主干：「产业迅猛发展」= 主谓；「出口量位居首位」是伴随结果。
- 处理：后半句用 `with + 名词 + 现在分词` 的复合结构（`with its export volume ranking first worldwide`）降级，避免两个主谓并列造成 run-on sentence。
- 时态：「近年来」搭配现在完成时 `has developed`。
- 译文：*In recent years, China's drone industry has developed rapidly, with its export volume ranking first worldwide.*

**第 2 句**：中国无人机凭借其出色的稳定性、高清成像能力和便捷的操作性，深受全球消费者的青睐，在民用领域得到了广泛应用。
- 主干：「深受青睐」「得到应用」两个并列谓语 → `are highly popular ... and widely applied ...`。
- 处理：「凭借……」用现在分词短语 `Boasting ...` 置于句首作状语，比 `with` 更有表现力；三个「……性/能力」并列作宾语，词性保持一致（名词短语）。
- 译文：*Boasting outstanding stability, high-definition imaging capability and easy operation, Chinese drones are highly popular among global consumers and widely applied in civilian sectors.*

**第 3 句**：作为国家战略性新兴产业，无人机正逐步演变为智能助手，满足多样化场景的需求。
- 主干：「无人机正演变为智能助手」→ `drones are evolving into intelligent assistants`。
- 处理：「作为……」介词短语前置；「满足……需求」用形容词短语 `suitable for diverse scenarios` 作后置定语，收尾更简洁。
- 译文：*As a national strategic emerging industry, drones are evolving into intelligent assistants suitable for diverse scenarios.*

**第 4、5 句**：在农村地区，无人机能够精准识别农作物病虫害，并高效喷洒农药。在城市，它们能够快速配送新鲜农产品。
- 处理：两句结构平行（地点 + 能力 + 动作），用分号 `;` 连接成一句；第 5 句用 `they` 指代 drones，避免重复。
- 译文：*In rural areas, drones can accurately detect crop diseases and pests and spray pesticides efficiently; in cities, they can deliver fresh farm produce quickly.*

**第 6 句**：在不久的将来，无人机将在更多领域释放独特价值，为经济和社会进步注入新的动力。
- 主干：「释放价值」「注入动力」两个并列谓语 → `will generate ... and inject ...`。
- 时态：「在不久的将来」→ 一般将来时 `will`。
- 译文：*In the near future, drones will generate unique value in more fields and inject new momentum into economic and social development.*

#### 【重点词汇】

| 中文 | 英文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| 无人机 | drone / unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) | 首现写全称可加注 UAV |
| 产业 | industry | drone industry 无人机产业 |
| 出口量 | export volume | volume 表「量」 |
| 位居首位 | rank first | rank first worldwide 全球第一 |
| 稳定性 | stability | 名词，-ity 结尾 |
| 高清成像能力 | high-definition imaging capability | high-definition 高清的 |
| 操作 | operation | easy operation 操作简便 |
| 消费者 | consumer | 复数 consumers |
| 民用领域 | civilian sectors / civilian fields | 反义 military 军用 |
| 战略性新兴产业 | strategic emerging industry | 固定政策表达，必背 |
| 智能助手 | intelligent assistant | intelligent  Intelligent 拼写 |
| 多样化的场景 | diverse scenarios | diverse = varied |
| 农作物病虫害 | crop diseases and pests | pest 害虫 |
| 喷洒农药 | spray pesticides | spray 喷洒 |
| 生鲜农产品 | fresh farm produce | produce 作名词读 /ˈproʊdjuːs/ |
| 新动能 | new momentum | momentum 不可数 |

#### 【重点短语】

- **In recent years** — 近年来（配现在完成时）
- **rank first worldwide** — 位居全球首位
- **be popular among** — 深受……青睐
- **be widely applied in** — 广泛应用于
- **evolve into** — 逐步演变为
- **be suitable for** — 适合 / 适配于（后接名词或动名词）
- **in the near future** — 在不久的将来
- **generate unique value** — 释放独特价值
- **inject new momentum into** — 为……注入新动能

### 2026.6-2 农村电子商务（直播带货）

#### 【中文原题】

电子商务的蓬勃发展正在深刻改变中国农村的面貌。随着互联网和物流网络迅速向农村地区延伸，越来越多的农村居民通过电子商务平台直接向城市消费者销售农产品。近年来，直播带货让城市消费者能够与卖家实时互动，成为推动农村发展的新途径。电子商务打破了城乡之间的信息壁垒，拓宽了农产品的销售渠道，帮助农村居民增加收入，改善生活。数字经济与乡村振兴的深度融合，为中国农村的发展注入了前所未有的活力。

#### 【参考译文】

The rapid development of e-commerce is profoundly changing the appearance of China's rural areas. As the internet and logistics networks quickly expand into the countryside, a growing number of rural residents are selling agricultural products directly to urban consumers through e-commerce platforms. In recent years, live-streaming e-commerce has enabled city consumers to interact with sellers in real time, becoming a new way to promote rural development. E-commerce has broken down the information barrier between urban and rural areas, expanded the sales channels of agricultural products, and helped rural residents increase their incomes and improve their lives. The deep integration of the digital economy with rural revitalization has injected unprecedented vitality into the development of China's rural areas.

#### 【逐句拆解】

**第 1 句**：电子商务的蓬勃发展正在深刻改变中国农村的面貌。
- 主干：「蓬勃发展」作主语中心词 → `The rapid development of e-commerce`；「正在改变」用现在进行时 `is changing`。
- 用词：「面貌」译 `appearance` 即可，也可写 `the face of rural China`（更地道）；「深刻」用副词 `profoundly`。
- 译文：*The rapid development of e-commerce is profoundly changing the appearance of China's rural areas.*

**第 2 句**：随着互联网和物流网络迅速向农村地区延伸，越来越多的农村居民通过电子商务平台直接向城市消费者销售农产品。
- 处理：「随着……」用 `As` 引导的状语从句前置，把背景信息交代清楚。
- 主干：「居民销售农产品」→ `residents are selling agricultural products`；「通过电商平台」「直接」「向城市消费者」依次作方式、程度、对象状语。
- 译文：*As the internet and logistics networks quickly expand into the countryside, a growing number of rural residents are selling agricultural products directly to urban consumers through e-commerce platforms.*

**第 3 句**：近年来，直播带货让城市消费者能够与卖家实时互动，成为推动农村发展的新途径。
- 主干：「直播带货让……能够互动」→ `live-streaming e-commerce has enabled city consumers to interact ...`；`enable sb. to do` 是「使……能够」的高频句型。
- 处理：「成为……新途径」用现在分词短语 `becoming a new way to ...` 作结果状语，接在主句后，避免另起一句。
- 译文：*In recent years, live-streaming e-commerce has enabled city consumers to interact with sellers in real time, becoming a new way to promote rural development.*

**第 4 句**：电子商务打破了城乡之间的信息壁垒，拓宽了农产品的销售渠道，帮助农村居民增加收入，改善生活。
- 处理：中文「打破、拓宽、帮助」三个并列动词，英文用三个并列谓语 `has broken down ... , expanded ... , and helped ...`，共用一个主语 e-commerce。
- 用词：「信息壁垒」`information barrier`；「销售渠道」`sales channels`；「增加收入、改善生活」`increase their incomes and improve their lives`。
- 译文：*E-commerce has broken down the information barrier between urban and rural areas, expanded the sales channels of agricultural products, and helped rural residents increase their incomes and improve their lives.*

**第 5 句**：数字经济与乡村振兴的深度融合，为中国农村的发展注入了前所未有的活力。
- 主干：「深度融合」作主语 → `The deep integration of the digital economy with rural revitalization`；「注入活力」→ `has injected unprecedented vitality into ...`。
- 用词：`inject ... into ...` 是发展类话题的高分搭配；「前所未有的」`unprecedented`（注意拼写）。
- 译文：*The deep integration of the digital economy with rural revitalization has injected unprecedented vitality into the development of China's rural areas.*

#### 【重点词汇】

| 中文 | 英文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| 电子商务 | e-commerce | 固定词，连字符 |
| 蓬勃发展 | rapid / booming development | booming 更突出活跃态势 |
| 农村地区 | rural areas / the countryside | 反义 urban 城市的 |
| 物流网络 | logistics networks | logistics 物流 |
| 居民 | resident | rural residents 农村居民 |
| 农产品 | agricultural products / farm produce | 两种都可 |
| 直播带货 | live-streaming e-commerce / livestreaming commerce | 主题高频词 |
| 实时互动 | interact in real time | real time 作副词 |
| 信息壁垒 | information barrier | break down a barrier 打破壁垒 |
| 销售渠道 | sales channels | channel 渠道 |
| 深度融合 | deep integration | integrate v. / integration n. |
| 数字经济 | digital economy | 政策类高频词 |
| 乡村振兴 | rural revitalization | 固定政策表达，必背 |
| 前所未有的 | unprecedented | 注意拼写 u-n-p-r-e-c-e-d-e-n-t-e-d |
| 活力 | vitality | 不可数 |

#### 【重点短语】

- **the appearance / face of ...** — ……的面貌
- **a growing number of** — 越来越多的（后接可数复数）
- **enable sb. to do sth.** — 使某人能够做某事
- **in real time** — 实时地
- **break down the information barrier** — 打破信息壁垒
- **increase one's income** — 增加某人收入
- **the deep integration of A with B** — A 与 B 的深度融合
- **inject ... vitality into ...** — 为……注入……活力
- **a new way to do sth. / a new driver of** — ……的新途径 / 新引擎

### 2026.6-3 科技医疗（远程医疗）

#### 【中文原题】

科学技术的进步推动了我国医疗行业的快速发展。近年来，远程医疗技术使得优质医疗资源得以惠及更多患者。医疗专家可通过视频通话方式向偏远地区的患者提供咨询服务，并指导当地医生制定并实施治疗方案。人工智能辅助诊断系统帮助医生识别患者的早期症状，并提高诊断的准确性。电子健康档案的普及让医生能够快速了解患者病史，避免重复检查。线上医疗咨询平台极大地方便了患者就诊。科技与医疗的深度融合更好地满足了人民的健康需求。

#### 【参考译文】

Advances in science and technology have led to rapid development in China's medical industry. In recent years, telemedicine has made high-quality medical resources available to more patients. Medical experts can provide consultations for patients in remote areas via video and guide local doctors to develop and carry out treatment plans. AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify early symptoms of patients and improve diagnostic accuracy. The widespread use of electronic health records allows doctors to quickly understand patients' medical history and avoid unnecessary repeated tests. Online medical consultation platforms have made it much easier for patients to see a doctor. The deep integration of technology and healthcare has better met the health needs of the people.

#### 【逐句拆解】

**第 1 句**：科学技术的进步推动了我国医疗行业的快速发展。
- 主干：「进步推动了发展」→ `Advances ... have led to rapid development`。
- 用词：「科学技术的进步」用 `Advances in science and technology`（比 the progress 更书面）；「推动」用 `led to / driven`。
- 译文：*Advances in science and technology have led to rapid development in China's medical industry.*

**第 2 句**：近年来，远程医疗技术使得优质医疗资源得以惠及更多患者。
- 主干：`telemedicine has made high-quality medical resources available to more patients`。
- 句型：`make + 宾语 + 形容词宾补`（make sth. available to sb.），是「使……得以被……」的地道处理。
- 译文：*In recent years, telemedicine has made high-quality medical resources available to more patients.*

**第 3 句**：医疗专家可通过视频通话方式向偏远地区的患者提供咨询服务，并指导当地医生制定并实施治疗方案。
- 主干：「提供咨询」「指导医生」两个并列谓语 → `provide consultations ... and guide local doctors ...`。
- 用词：「远程地区患者」`patients in remote areas`；「制定并实施治疗方案」`develop and carry out treatment plans`（搭配 plan 的固定动词）。
- 译文：*Medical experts can provide consultations for patients in remote areas via video and guide local doctors to develop and carry out treatment plans.*

**第 4 句**：人工智能辅助诊断系统帮助医生识别患者的早期症状，并提高诊断的准确性。
- 主干：`AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify ... and improve ...`。
- 用词：「人工智能辅助诊断系统」`AI-assisted diagnostic systems`；「早期症状」`early symptoms`；「诊断准确率」`diagnostic accuracy`。
- 译文：*AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify early symptoms of patients and improve diagnostic accuracy.*

**第 5 句**：电子健康档案的普及让医生能够快速了解患者病史，避免重复检查。
- 主干：「普及让医生能够……」→ `The widespread use of electronic health records allows doctors to ...`。
- 用词：「电子健康档案」`electronic health records`；「病史」`medical history`；「重复检查」`repeated tests`。
- 译文：*The widespread use of electronic health records allows doctors to quickly understand patients' medical history and avoid unnecessary repeated tests.*

**第 6 句**：线上医疗咨询平台极大地方便了患者就诊。
- 句型：`make it + 形容词 + to do` 形式宾语结构 → `have made it much easier for patients to see a doctor`。
- 译文：*Online medical consultation platforms have made it much easier for patients to see a doctor.*

**第 7 句**：科技与医疗的深度融合更好地满足了人民的健康需求。
- 主干：「融合满足了需求」→ `The deep integration ... has better met the health needs ...`。
- 用词：`the deep integration of technology and healthcare`（深度融合）；`meet one's needs`（满足需求）。
- 译文：*The deep integration of technology and healthcare has better met the health needs of the people.*

#### 【重点词汇】

| 中文 | 英文 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|
| 医疗行业 | medical industry / healthcare sector | healthcare 医疗（总称） |
| 远程医疗 | telemedicine | 主题核心词，必背 |
| 优质医疗资源 | high-quality medical resources | high-quality 作定语 |
| 患者 | patient | 复数 patients |
| 偏远地区 | remote areas | remote 偏远的 |
| 咨询 | consultation | provide consultations 提供咨询 |
| 治疗方案 | treatment plan | develop / carry out a plan |
| 人工智能辅助诊断系统 | AI-assisted diagnostic systems | AI-assisted 作复合定语 |
| 早期症状 | early symptoms | symptom 症状 |
| 诊断准确率 | diagnostic accuracy | accuracy 准确性 |
| 电子健康档案 | electronic health records | 固定术语 |
| 病史 | medical history | history 此处不可数 |
| 深度融合 | deep integration | integrate v. |
| 健康需求 | health needs | meet one's needs 满足需求 |

#### 【重点短语】

- **lead to** — 导致 / 推动
- **make sth. available to sb.** — 使某物可被某人获得 / 惠及某人
- **provide consultations for sb.** — 为某人提供咨询
- **carry out / develop a treatment plan** — 制定 / 实施治疗方案
- **make it + adj. + for sb. to do** — 使某人做某事变得……
- **see a doctor** — 就诊 / 看病
- **the integration of A and B** — A 与 B 的融合
- **meet one's needs** — 满足某人的需求

### 2025.12-1 尊老敬老

#### 【中文原题】

尊老是中华民族的传统美德，深深植根于中国人的思想和行为中，是人们普遍遵守的行为规范和社会准则。在当今的中国，这种美德得到广泛传承。社会各界积极营造敬老助老的社会氛围。为老年人提供便利服务已成为社会共识，例如社区专门开设长者食堂，公共场所配置优先座位。政府还出台了一系列政策，为老年人的权益提供有力保障。尊老是社会和谐与发展的重要基石，有助于培育良好的社会氛围，推动整个社会文明进步。

#### 【参考译文】

Respecting the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted in the thoughts and actions of the Chinese people. It is a widely followed code of conduct and social norm. In modern China, this virtue has been widely passed down. All sectors of society actively foster a social atmosphere of respecting and helping the elderly. Providing convenient services for the elderly has become a social consensus. For example, communities have specially set up canteens for senior citizens, and priority seats are arranged in public places. The government has also introduced a series of policies to provide strong safeguards for the rights and interests of the elderly. Respecting the elderly serves as an important foundation for social harmony and development. It helps create a positive social atmosphere and promotes the civilized progress of the whole society.

#### 【逐句拆解】

**第 1 句**：尊老是中华民族的传统美德，深深植根于中国人的思想和行为中，是人们普遍遵守的行为规范和社会准则。
- 主干：`Respecting the elderly is a traditional virtue ...`（动名词作主语）。
- 处理：「深深植根于……」用过去分词短语 `deeply rooted in ...` 作后置定语；「是人们普遍遵守的……」另起 `It is a widely followed code of conduct and social norm`。
- 译文：*Respecting the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted in the thoughts and actions of the Chinese people. It is a widely followed code of conduct and social norm.*

**第 2 句**：在当今的中国，这种美德得到广泛传承。

- 用词：`pass down`（代代传承）区别于 `pass on`（普通传递）。

For example, communities have specially set up canteens for senior citizens, and priority seats are arranged in public places.*

**第 5 句**：政府还出台了一系列政策，为老年人的权益提供有力保障。
- 主干：`The government has also introduced a series of policies to provide strong safeguards ...`。
- 用词：「出台政策」`introduce / implement policies`；「权益」`rights and interests`；「有力保障」`strong / robust safeguards`。
- 译文：*The government has also introduced a series of policies to provide strong safeguards for the rights and interests of the elderly.*

**第 6、7 句**：尊老是社会和谐与发展的重要基石，有助于培育良好的社会氛围，推动整个社会文明进步。
- 主干：`Respecting the elderly serves as an important foundation ...`。
- 处理：「有助于……推动……」用 `It helps create ... and promotes ...`；「基石」`foundation / cornerstone`。
- 译文：*Respecting the elderly serves as an important foundation for social harmony and development. It helps create a positive social atmosphere and promotes the civilized progress of the whole society.*

### 2024.6-1 中国传统婚礼习俗

#### 【中文原题】

#### 【参考译文】

China's traditional wedding customs have a long history. A complete set of wedding ceremonies gradually took shape as early as the Zhou Dynasty, some of which have been followed to this day. #### 【逐句拆解】

- 处理：三个动作分层——主干 `customs have a long history`；「形成仪式」`a complete set of wedding ceremonies gradually took shape as early as the Zhou Dynasty`（过去时，讲历史起源）；「有些沿用至今」用定语从句 `some of which have been followed to this day`（现在完成时被动）。
- 译文：*China's traditional wedding customs have a long history. A complete set of wedding ceremonies gradually took shape as early as the Zhou Dynasty, some of which have been followed to this day.*

**第 2 句**：如今的中式婚礼习俗已有很大变化，但婚礼庆典仍然十分隆重。
- 处理：转折结构 but 连接两个主干；「已有很大变化」现在完成时 `have changed greatly`，「仍然十分隆重」一般现在时 `remains very grand`。

### 2024.6-2 中国竹子

#### 【中文原题】

中国盛产竹子，是最早开发利用竹资源的国家。竹子在中国分布广泛，品种丰富。竹子实用性强，用于生产和生活的许多方面，如筷子、桌椅的制作和桥梁、房屋的建造。中国人爱竹，自古以来就有无数文人以竹为主题，创作了绚丽多彩的文学和绘画作品。竹子主干 (stem) 笔直，象征正直的品格。竹子具有强大的生命力和适应能力，无论环境多么恶劣，都能够顽强生存，因而寓意坚韧不拔的精神。几千年来，竹子一直被视为中华民族品格的象征。

#### 【参考译文】

It is also highly practical and is used in many aspects of production and daily life, such as making chopsticks, tables and chairs, and building bridges and houses. The Chinese people love bamboo, and since ancient times countless scholars have created splendid literary and artistic works with bamboo as their theme. The straight stem of bamboo symbolizes an upright character. Moreover, bamboo has strong vitality and adaptability; no matter how harsh the environment is, it can survive tenaciously, thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance. For thousands of years, bamboo has been regarded as a symbol of the character of the Chinese nation.

#### 【逐句拆解】

**第 1 句**：中国盛产竹子，是最早开发利用竹资源的国家。
- 主干：`China abounds in bamboo and is among the first countries ...`（共用主语 China，两个谓语）。
- 用词：「盛产」`abounds in / is rich in`（abound 不及物，必须接 in）；「最早……的国家」用不定式后置定语 `the first countries to develop and utilize bamboo resources`。
- 译文：*China abounds in bamboo and is among the first countries to develop and utilize bamboo resources.*

**第 2 句**：竹子在中国分布广泛，品种丰富。
- 处理：两个并列描述，`Bamboo is widely distributed across China and comes in a rich variety of species`。
- 用词：「分布」用被动 `is distributed`；bamboo 作植物总称不可数，谓语用单数。
- 译文：*Bamboo is widely distributed across China and comes in a rich variety of species.*

**第 3 句**：竹子实用性强，用于生产和生活的许多方面，如筷子、桌椅的制作和桥梁、房屋的建造。
- 处理：「实用性强」`is highly practical`；「用于」被动 `is used in`；「如……的制作/建造」用 such as + 动名词 `such as making chopsticks ... and building bridges ...`。
- 译文：*It is also highly practical and is used in many aspects of production and daily life, such as making chopsticks, tables and chairs, and building bridges and houses.*

**第 4 句**：中国人爱竹，自古以来就有无数文人以竹为主题，创作了绚丽多彩的文学和绘画作品。
- 处理：两套主语——`The Chinese people love bamboo` 和 `countless scholars have created splendid literary and artistic works`；「自古以来」`since ancient times` 触发现在完成时；「以竹为主题」`with bamboo as their theme`。
- 译文：*The Chinese people love bamboo, and since ancient times countless scholars have created splendid literary and artistic works with bamboo as their theme.*

**第 5 句**：竹子主干笔直，象征正直的品格。
- 处理：把「笔直」提为定语、「象征」作谓语更凝练：`The straight stem of bamboo symbolizes an upright character`。
- 用词：原文给提示词 stem；「正直」用 upright（不用 straight，那是物理笔直）。

- 处理：主干 `bamboo has strong vitality and adaptability`；「无论……恶劣」让步状语 `no matter how harsh the environment is`（陈述语序）；「因而寓意……」用 `thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance` 作结果状语。
- 译文：*Moreover, bamboo has strong vitality and adaptability; no matter how harsh the environment is, it can survive tenaciously, thus symbolizing the spirit of perseverance.*
